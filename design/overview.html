How these figures are calculated

Recorded usage is Desktop-ledger usage priced at the rates captured when each delta was recorded (1–18 May in this synthetic set). It records what usage was priced at — it is not a provider bill, invoice, or proof of payment. API-equivalent re-prices backend usage at the currently available catalogue over 19 Apr – 18 May — a comparison estimate, never a charge, and a lower bound while any tokens are unpriced. Declared fees are the recurring amounts you confirmed in Memberships, kept separate by currency and period; Spend Sense does not verify them against a bill. Subscription value compares attributed usage value with fees only when window and currency align; otherwise the card shows Not available with the reason.

Full per-model detail lives in Usage; fee evidence and economics live in Costs. Nothing here sends data anywhere.

Synthetic reference for design review (issue #13). Figures do not come from any real account. In production these cards bind to settledDesktopMonth, backend analytics totals, membershipSummary and the economics headline — with the same labels and windows.
